# Chirality (Study Notes)

## Definition

**Chirality** is the property of an object or molecule that **cannot be superimposed on its mirror image**.

**Example:** Left and right hands.

---

## Chiral Compound

A **chiral compound** is a compound whose mirror image is **different** and **cannot be perfectly overlapped** with the original molecule.

The two mirror-image forms are called **enantiomers**.

---

## Chiral Centre (Stereogenic Centre)

A **chiral centre** is usually a **carbon atom bonded to four different substituents**.

Example:

```
      OH
       |
CH3 — C — H
       |
      Cl
```

Since all four groups are different, this carbon is chiral.

---

## Conditions for Chirality

A molecule is generally chiral if:

* It has at least one stereogenic (chiral) centre.
* It has **no internal plane of symmetry**.
* Its mirror image cannot be superimposed.

---

## Achiral Compound

An **achiral compound** is **superimposable on its mirror image**.

Examples:

* Methane (CH₄)
* Carbon dioxide (CO₂)
* Benzene

---

## Enantiomers

* Mirror-image forms of a chiral molecule.
* Same molecular formula.
* Same connectivity.
* Different three-dimensional arrangement.
* Rotate plane-polarized light in opposite directions.

---

## Absolute Configuration

The exact three-dimensional arrangement around a chiral centre is called its **absolute configuration**.

It is designated using:

* **R (Rectus)**
* **S (Sinister)**

---

## Determination of Absolute Configuration

The most reliable method is:

✅ **X-ray diffraction (X-ray crystallography)**

Reason:
It determines the **three-dimensional positions of atoms**, allowing assignment of the correct R/S configuration.

---

## Importance of Chirality

Many biological molecules are chiral.

Examples:

* Amino acids
* Sugars
* Proteins
* DNA

Different enantiomers can have **completely different biological effects**.

Example:
One enantiomer of a drug may be therapeutic, while the other may be inactive or even harmful.
